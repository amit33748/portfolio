About

From field to satellite

I work on the analysis end of remote sensing: taking SAR stacks, stereo pairs and drone imagery and turning them into maps that a department can act on.

At HARSAC (Haryana Space Applications Centre) I derive Sentinel-1 PSInSAR LOS velocity for the whole state, generate DEMs, DSMs and orthophotos of mining sites in Trimble Inpho UAS Master, and run time-series crop mapping with semi-physical rice yield estimation.

Field work is part of the job: DGPS control, LAI and chlorophyll readings that keep the satellite products honest.

Projects

Research & builds

01

Land Subsidence Monitoring in Haryana using Sentinel-1 PSInSAR

State-scale Persistent Scatterer InSAR built from 36 Sentinel-1 images, producing LOS velocity for the whole of Haryana. The results are published on a public HARSAC web portal where anyone can drill down to a village and read its deformation time series.

PSInSAR Sentinel-1 36 scenes LOS velocity Web GIS
02

Land Subsidence Monitoring in Kolkata using Sentinel-1 PSInSAR

Applied Persistent Scatterer InSAR to a 21-scene Sentinel-1 stack spanning 2019 to 2024, processed in StaMPS on MATLAB. The resulting LOS velocity and deformation maps isolate subsidence-prone zones relevant to urban infrastructure planning.

PSInSAR StaMPS MATLAB Sentinel-1 SNAP
Download Full Report (PDF, 5.8 MB)
03

Landslide Susceptibility Zonation of West & South Sikkim using AHP

Built an Analytical Hierarchy Process weighted multi-criteria model in GIS to rank landslide susceptibility, then produced zonation maps with recommendations for disaster management and land-use planning.

AHP Multi-criteria ArcGIS Hazard zonation
04

AI-Driven LinkedIn Post Automation

An n8n workflow that takes a plain-language instruction from a Telegram bot, drafts the post with the open-source Qwen model served locally through the LM Studio API, and sends it back to Telegram for human approval before publishing to LinkedIn. No paid cloud LLM API.
